# Flight Crew Alertness: Support

Flight Crew Alertness is the Alliance Airlines app for submitting crew alertness and fatigue reports from a company iPad.

This app is for Alliance Airlines employees only and is installed through company-managed devices.

## Getting help

If you have a problem with the app, please contact the Alliance Airlines IT team:

- Email: itservicedesk@allianceairlines.com.au


When you get in touch, it helps to include:

- What you were doing when the problem happened
- Any message shown on screen
- The app version (found in the app's settings or on the App Store listing)

Please do not include any personal or sensitive details in your message.

## Common questions

**Do I need to sign in?**
No. The app does not use accounts or sign-in.

**Is my report anonymous?**
Reports are not linked to an individual crew member. See the privacy policy below for details.
